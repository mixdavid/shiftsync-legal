Generic fasting apps assume you sleep at night. You don't.

Eating at the wrong biological hour is linked to worse sleep and metabolic strain. A 16:8 window that starts at 8 AM is useless when your shift starts at 11 PM. ShiftSync plans against your rotation, not a 9-to-5 clock.

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

Set your roster once. ShiftSync translates every shift into a plan for eating, caffeine, and sleep.

1

Paint your roster

Day, night, swing, off. Tap shifts onto a calendar, or set a repeating rotation like 2 days / 2 nights / 4 off and let it fill itself.

2

Get a daily plan

Each shift becomes an eating window, a caffeine cutoff, and a sleep target. After a night block, you get a lighter recovery day instead of a pretend-normal schedule.

3

Run your fast

One tap starts today's fast. A live ring tracks progress, widgets keep it on your Home Screen, and reminders never wake you mid-sleep.
