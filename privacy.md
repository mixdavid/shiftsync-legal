# Privacy Policy for ShiftSync

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

ShiftSync ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ShiftSync iOS application.

## 1. Local Data Storage
ShiftSync does not require account registration and does not operate a remote database for your personal routines. Your shift schedules, fasting timer logs, and app preferences are stored **locally on your device** and shared locally with the ShiftSync Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets via Apple's App Groups framework. We do not have access to, collect, or sell your shift or fasting data.

## 2. Subscriptions & Payment Processing
All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed exclusively by **Apple** through the App Store. We do not collect or have access to your credit card or Apple ID billing details.

To validate subscription receipts and unlock Pro features, ShiftSync uses **RevenueCat** (`RevenueCat, Inc.`). RevenueCat processes anonymized purchase receipts and an anonymous App User ID solely to manage subscription entitlements. RevenueCat does not receive your shift schedules or fasting logs. You can review RevenueCat's privacy practices at [https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## 3. Notifications
If you grant permission, ShiftSync schedules **local notifications** on your device to remind you of upcoming fasting and eating windows. Notification scheduling happens entirely on-device.

## 4. Tracking & Advertising
ShiftSync does not display third-party advertisements, does not use cross-app tracking (IDFA), and does not sell or rent user data to third parties.

## 5. Data Deletion
Because your schedule and fasting history are stored locally on your device, uninstalling ShiftSync from your iPhone permanently removes all local app data.

## 6. Children's Privacy
ShiftSync is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at **mixmckagan@hotmail.com**.
