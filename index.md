## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I restore my Pro subscription?
Open **ShiftSync** → tap **Settings** → tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for your original purchase.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly through Apple:
1. Open the iOS **Settings** app.
2. Tap your **Apple ID** name at the top.
3. Tap **Subscriptions** → select **ShiftSync**.
4. Choose a different plan or tap **Cancel Subscription**.

### Why aren't my notifications or widgets updating?
- **Notifications:** Go to iOS **Settings → Notifications → ShiftSync** and verify that **Allow Notifications** is enabled.
- **Widgets:** After editing your shift schedule, return to the Today tab once to ensure the latest countdown syncs immediately with your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
